About This Project

An overview of fake review detection concepts, NLP techniques, and machine learning methodology.

What is fake review detection?

Fake review detection uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) and machine learning to identify subtle textual patterns in review text that may distinguish suspicious, computer-generated, or incentivized reviews from genuine customer feedback.

Online marketplaces face millions of user-submitted reviews daily. Automated ML classification models assist in flagging suspicious language, repetitive phrasings, and abnormal structural patterns before manual moderation.

Component Overview

NLP (Natural Language Processing)

Used to clean and standardize human language input. Raw reviews are lowercased, stripped of HTML tags, punctuation, and non-alphanumeric noise, then tokenized to remove non-informative English stopwords.

TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency)

Converts text strings into numerical feature vectors. It evaluates word importance by balancing how frequently a word occurs in a specific review against how commonly it appears across the entire review dataset (using unigrams and bigrams).

Logistic Regression

Serves as the binary classification algorithm. It calculates the decision boundary and probability score estimating whether a review is Likely Genuine or Likely Fake based on learned TF-IDF feature weights.
